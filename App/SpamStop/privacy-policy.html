Privacy Policy for SpamStop

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

1. Introduction

ACALI Studios ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile application SpamStop. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

2. Data Collection and Use

SMS Messages: SpamStop reads incoming SMS messages solely to determine whether they should be filtered as spam. Message content is evaluated on-device and is never transmitted to any server or third party.

Contacts: SpamStop reads your device contacts to build a whitelist, ensuring messages from people you know are never filtered. Contact data is accessed locally and is not stored, shared, or transmitted.

Local Storage: App settings (category toggles, custom keywords, auto-reply preferences) and a log of filtered messages are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device.

Personal Data: We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

3. SMS Permissions

SpamStop uses Android's SMS filtering API (CallScreeningService / SMS Verification API) to intercept and evaluate incoming messages. The App does not read, store, or transmit your personal conversations — only messages classified as spam by the filter are logged within the app on your device.

If the Auto-Reply STOP feature is enabled, SpamStop may send an SMS reply on your behalf to political or marketing senders. You are always in control of this feature and can disable it at any time in the app settings.

4. No Cloud, No Accounts

SpamStop requires no account creation and does not connect to any remote server. All filtering decisions are made entirely on your device. No message content, contact information, or usage data is ever uploaded or shared.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Play Services, which may collect information used to identify you. We encourage you to review the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

SpamStop is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective when posted to this page.
