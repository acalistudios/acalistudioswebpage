Privacy Policy for RetroTAP

Last Updated: May 27, 2026

1. Introduction

ACALI Studios ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile game RetroTAP. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Game.

2. Data Collection and Use

Local Data: RetroTAP stores game-related data, such as high scores, player initials, and game settings, locally on your device using industry-standard persistence (SharedPreferences). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Personal Data: We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

3. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Advertising)

RetroTAP displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the following data to serve ads:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable device identifier used to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • App interactions — information about how you interact with ads in the app.
  • Crash logs, diagnostics, and app performance data — used to maintain and improve ad delivery.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK, you will be shown a consent form when you first launch the app. You may choose to receive personalized ads or non-personalized ads. You can review or update your choice at any time in the app's settings.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of interest-based advertising at any time in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

RetroTAP offers optional in-app purchases processed through Google Play Billing. All payment transactions are handled entirely by Google. We do not collect or store any payment or financial information. Please review Google Play's Terms of Service for details.

Google Play Services

The Game uses Google Play Services, which may collect information used to identify you. We encourage you to review the Google Play Services Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

RetroTAP is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to remove that information.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
