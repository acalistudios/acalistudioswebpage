SpamStop

Intelligent SMS and MMS filtering. Your inbox, protected.

What It Does

SpamStop silences unwanted text messages — political campaigns, marketing blasts, prize scams, and fraud attempts — across both SMS and MMS. It reads and classifies each message on your phone, suppresses the notification when it matches, and files the message in a private log you can review. Your original messages stay exactly where they are, in your usual messaging app. Contacts you've saved are always let through.

No accounts, no cloud, no analytics. Filtering happens entirely on your device, and nothing is sent to us. The one exception is under your control: if you choose to report a scam to your carrier at 7726, that message is forwarded to your carrier — and only then. See the Privacy Policy for details.
