SpamStop

Intelligent SMS filtering. Your inbox, protected.

What It Does

SpamStop silently intercepts unwanted text messages before they reach you — political campaigns, marketing blasts, prize scams, and phishing attempts. Contacts you've saved are always let through. No accounts, no cloud, no data leaving your phone.

🔒

Category Blocking

Toggle off entire categories — Political, Scam, Marketing, and Phishing — with a single switch.

👥

Contact Whitelist

Messages from your saved contacts are never filtered, no matter what they say.

📜

Custom Keywords

Block any message containing words or phrases you define. Your rules, your inbox.

🛑

Auto-Reply STOP

Automatically sends an unsubscribe reply to political and marketing senders on your behalf.
